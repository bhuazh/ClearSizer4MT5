ClearSizer MT5 v1.80 — validation record
2026-10-30

Changes:
- Right-anchored forecast labels replace the horizontal lines' left descriptions.
  SL shows price, total lots and total estimated loss. Each TP shows its own price,
  leg volume and leg cash result. Entry summarizes price, total lots, total SL
  risk and combined TP profit. All money is in account currency, including costs.
- CSQuote records the existing per-leg risk/reward calculations in arrays;
  volume sizing, broker requests and order validation retain their algorithms.
- Display-only quote copies supply size previews while drawing. Pending SL with
  no Entry shows unavailable values. Hypothetical missing TP cash is never shown:
  Entry TP total requires real ready targets, and visible TP0 uses its real price.
- Failed quotes display --. Submitted forecasts freeze Entry, lines/zones, cash
  and target count. New Order/Quick unlock the next draft; BE remains available.
- Labels use measured full/compact/price-only text, avoid the panel and main-chart
  edges, and separate nearby levels vertically. Full data remains in tooltips.
  Layout changes refresh placement against this frame's panel bounds and fonts.
- Hovering a label keeps its candidate price fixed. Clicking it commits the
  displayed price rather than its offset text coordinate. Fixed labels cannot
  advance another drawing stage; existing click deduplication remains in force.
- Tag objects survive body redraws and are removed with cancellation/new drafts.

Retained v1.70 behavior:
- Footer contains exactly two visible dynamic lines: status and detail. Complete
  status/detail/context is retained in both tooltips. Errors, results and explicit
  confirmations retain priority. The old hint object is removed.
- Default dimensions are 300x354 at 96 DPI/scale1, 32px shorter than v1.61.
  Each extra TP and own-exposure row still adds 28px. Footer reserves 80px on
  a single page and adds 20px only when navigation is needed.
- Red risk and blue reward rectangles span the visible main-chart plot.
  A shared viewport snapshot probes both edges, retreats past invalid borders
  or price scales and refines to the nearest valid main-plot pixels.
  Invalid dimensions/time coordinates remove stale bands for that update.
- Risk shading is present during SL/Entry/TP previews. Before pending Entry is
  placed, a local live-quote reference affects only the drawing. Blue reward
  shading starts with the TP preview and reaches the farthest enabled ready TP.
- Chart-change events and the existing timer refresh zone bounds. Bands are
  background, filled and nonselectable, below the price lines' click priority.

Retained v1.61 behavior:
- ESC now cancels every unsubmitted draft, including completed Ready setups and
  a pending subtype change waiting for reconfirmation. It clears draft lines,
  zones, prices and readiness; risk, target count and BE preferences remain.
  Submitted attempts retain their result and one-submission guard. ESC disarms
  close/cancel confirmations and never sends broker requests itself.
- The Ready hint now includes ESC to cancel. README explains HEDGING/NETTING.

Retained v1.60 behavior:
- NEW ORDER draws SL -> Entry -> TP and selects BUY/SELL from SL versus Entry.
  It automatically selects BUY LIMIT / BUY STOP against Ask or
  SELL LIMIT / SELL STOP against Bid. It never falls back to a market order.
- The first pending SL click is independent of the current quote. Entry and SL
  may both be above or below the market; SL may be inside the current spread.
  Pending SL/TP protection distances are checked against the planned Entry.
- QUICK retains its SL -> TP market workflow and current Bid/Ask SL constraints.
- Explicit Entry/SL edits reclassify pending orders. A side change clears old
  targets and requires TP1 to be placed again.
- The displayed pending subtype stays locked as quotes move. If CONFIRM sees
  a changed Limit/Stop subtype, it only updates the type and asks for another
  click; a 600ms guard prevents a queued double-click from reconfirming it.
- Dynamic drawing status stays below CANCEL SETUP / CONFIRM ORDER.
  Folded panel stays 300x38.
- Retained: one page, TP2/TP3/BE in one row, target fields above those buttons,
  fixed 1R/zero-offset BE without trailing, X removal and reload-safe lock,
  one actual submission per draft, and contextual scoped cancel/close controls.
